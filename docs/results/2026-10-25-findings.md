# What personality can be read from self-descriptions: findings from PDB, Essays and PANDORA

*2026-10-25. Aggregate results only. Each number links back to a tool and a dated results report in `docs/results/`.*

## Summary

- **Typology talk is the biggest confound.** Three in four PDB profiles talk about typology, and that talk works as a
  hidden label. Removing it costs an LLM 12 points of exact-type accuracy, and an agent that reads it copies the
  self-type rather than reading the person.
- **With leakage removed, text predicts MBTI type about 30% of the time** (chance is about 8%). A zero-shot LLM reading
  as a PDB voter gets there; small local models trained on ~800 profiles reach 15-18%, and training them on the LLM's
  labels does not close the gap.
- **PDB's crowd types with cognitive-function theory; text models don't.** Voters' runner-up types are function
  neighbours; models confuse along single letters, mostly J/P.
- **Big Five from text transfers to real self-report on self-reflective writing, not on chatter.** The local model
  matches published Essays models without seeing an essay (56.3%) and a zero-shot LLM beats them (59.8%, n=100), but on
  Reddit comments the signal is near zero.
- **Sensing is under-read everywhere** because the training population is about 75% intuitive; that prior, more than
  the text, decides the headline type.
- **LLM agents answering a type questionnaire drift towards INFP**, away from the type the same agent states.

## Data

| Source | What | Labels |
|---|---|---|
| Personality Database (PDB) "Pdb Profiles" | ~1,000 community members' profile pages: pinned and own comments, linked Google Docs | crowd votes in 15+ systems; the creator's own vote |
| Stage 3 agent battery | DeepSeek v4 flash role-plays each creator from their text (after Park et al. 2024) and completes IPIP-NEO-120 and MBTI Form M | Big Five domains and facets; Form M type |
| PDB-voter prompt | the same model asked to type the author as an experienced PDB voter would, on scrubbed text | 16-type probabilities, Big Five percentiles |
| Essays (Pennebaker & King 1999) | 2,467 student stream-of-consciousness essays | self-reported Big Five, median splits |
| PANDORA (Gjurković et al. 2021) | Reddit comments of 10,295 users | self-reported MBTI (9,084), Big Five (1,606), Enneagram (794) |

"Scrubbed" text has type codes and the talk around them removed (Enneagram, MBTI, instincts, cognitive functions,
"type N", partial codes). Local models: TF-IDF and gte-modernbert-base embedding halves averaged; 5-fold CV throughout.

## 1. Typology talk leaks the label

- Typology vocabulary appears in **755 of ~1,000 profiles** ("enneagram" 2,032 times, "sx" 1,095, "16personalities" 968).
  Its users are mostly voted N and P, so a 35-word intro about searching for one's Enneagram type flipped a
  description from ISFJ to INFP before the scrub was extended.
- **LLM voter, 25 profiles:** 48% exact on raw text vs 36% on scrubbed text.
- **The Stage 3 agent's own stated type** matches the creator's self-vote 55% of the time when the text names a type
  and 34% when it doesn't. The PDB-voter prompt on scrubbed text: 36% vs 33%.
- Claims that an AI "typed me right" need this control; most don't have it.

## 2. How much type is in the text

Against the crowd's modal type (chance exact about 8%):

| Reader | n | Exact | Top-3 | E/I | S/N | T/F | J/P |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Local models, crowd-trained (v0.3), full profiles | 778 | 17.7% | 43.6% | .62 | .73 | .66 | .59 |
| Local models, 250-word excerpts | 778 | 16.2% | 37.4% | .60 | .70 | .63 | .54 |
| DeepSeek as PDB voter, scrubbed, 1 sample | 785 | **29.8%** | **56.4%** | .67 | .73 | .72 | .69 |

Against the creator's own self-type, every leak-free method lands at 31-34% exact: Form M (prompt A 30%, v3b 33%) and
the voter prompt (31%). The crowd agrees with the creator 69% of the time, which is either accurate self-typing or
voters anchoring on the self-type shown on the page; this data can't separate the two.

## 3. The LLM's skill does not distil into small models

Local heads trained on the voter's 16-type distributions (778 profiles, 5-fold CV) score **13.6%** exact on excerpts
against 16.2% for crowd-trained heads, and agree with the teacher only 22-32% of the time. They copy its habits (S
recall on excerpts 27% to 50%, frequent ESFJ) but not its accuracy. Big Five heads trained on its estimates gain 0.4
points on Essays. With one noisy label per profile and under a thousand profiles, word and embedding features can't
reproduce a whole-profile, function-level reading.

## 4. The crowd reasons in functions; models in letters

Among one-letter neighbours, the share of each flip:

| | E/I | S/N | T/F | J/P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crowd: modal vs runner-up vote (420 profiles) | **30%** | 26% | 23% | **20%** |
| Jev: top-1 vs top-2 (683) | 27% | 20% | 23% | 30% |
| Local model: errors vs crowd (306) | 29% | 16% | 21% | 34% |

In function theory the E/I flip keeps both functions (INTP-ENTP) and the J/P flip keeps none (INTP-INTJ). Voters treat
mirror types as closest and J/P flips as furthest (p about .02); the models do the opposite. Training on the full vote
distribution instead of the modal vote did not change this (J/P share 34% to 33%).

## 5. Sensing is under-read

About 75% of crowd-typed profiles are N. Local models catch 9-27% of S types; the voter prompt, told the base rates and
warned against defaulting to N, catches 49%. On LLM-written portraits of all 16 types the local crowd card's headline
predicted no S type at all, even though its S/N letter head read them at .72-.78: the 16-type head's N-heavy prior
outvotes the letters. Dividing the prior out ("prior-neutral" headline) costs one point of exact accuracy on PDB and
predicts S about as often as the crowd does (214 vs 197 profiles); on the portraits it lifts top-3 from 53% to 72%. The
panel shows both headlines.

## 6. Questionnaire-answering agents drift towards INFP

The same agent's stated type and its Form M result agree only 41% of the time. Relative to the creator's self-type,
Form M answers move J to P (147 profiles), S to N (127), E to I (110) and T to F (71); the reverse moves are rare (N to
S: 7). Answering a questionnaire in character pulls towards the reflective, open-ended answer. Scrubbing the text (v3b)
halves the S-to-N drift but not the move to P and I.

## 7. Big Five: genre decides transfer

| Test (real self-report) | Local model | DeepSeek, zero-shot | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essays, median-split accuracy (2,467) | 56.3% (AUC .589) | **59.8%** (AUC .664, n=100) | Mairesse 2007: 56.7%; Majumder 2017 best: 58.8% (both trained on Essays) |
| PANDORA Reddit comments, mean Spearman r (500 users) | .06 | not run (terms forbid transfer) | external DistilBERT: -.03 |

- The local Big Five model is trained only on the agent's IPIP answers for PDB profiles, yet transfers to real
  self-report on all five traits in self-reflective essays. On Reddit chatter it carries little (O .18, N .12, C .10;
  E zero; A reversed).
- A published Hugging Face model claiming r .77 showed no signal on either test, most likely because of comment-level
  train/test splits.
- DeepSeek's zero-shot Essays result beats the best published Essays model; Neuroticism is its strongest trait (AUC .77).

## 8. PDB models on PANDORA's self-reported types

| Text (one document per user) | Users | Mean letter balanced acc. | T/F | Exact (best card) | Most common type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vent, advice, mental-health and personal-question subreddits | 4,264 | .54-.56 | .61 | 18.1% | 22.7% |
| Introduction posts (hsp, MakeNewFriendsHere, penpals, r4r, introvert) | 957 | .56-.59 | .63-.64 | 21.2% | 27.3% |
| Introductions without r/introvert | 332 | .57-.59 | .66-.69 | 21.1% | 19.0% |

T/F transfers; the other letters barely do. Short self-introductions read better than four times as much venting.
"I'm an introvert" in r/introvert carried most of the E/I signal (Jev E/I .65 with it, .57 without).

## 9. Enneagram observations

- **Core 3 vs core 1** (67 vs 32 crowd-typed profiles, agent IPIP facets, Benjamini-Hochberg corrected): 3s score
  higher on immoderation (d 1.25), excitement-seeking (1.23), gregariousness (.91) and adventurousness (.80); 1s on
  cautiousness (-1.05), dutifulness (-.90), modesty (-.86) and morality (-.85). Both are high in conscientiousness
  overall. Their scrubbed text barely differs (word-model AUC .59).
- **Social 5s** are not a distinct Big Five cluster: within core 5s only lower Neuroticism holds up (d -.60), and the
  five domains separate them from other 5s at AUC .60-.64, not significant.

## What did not help (pre-registered tests)

| Idea | Result |
|---|---|
| Psycholinguistic lexicon features (Mairesse 2007 style) | +0.5 Essays points, lost on PDB |
| Emotional-sentence filtering (Majumder 2017) | lost S/N on portraits (.75 to .56) |
| Training on vote distributions | better on full profiles, worse on excerpts |
| Agent Form M S/N as a teacher | within noise |
| Local models trained on LLM labels | lost 2.6 points exact |
| Jina v5 encoder | lost to e5-small, then gte-modernbert |

## Limitations

- PDB is a self-selected, N-heavy community of people interested in typology; its crowd votes are a social
  consensus, not a validated measure.
- The agent's IPIP answers are a model's reading of the text, not the creator's own scores; Big Five numbers against
  them measure agreement with that reading. Essays and PANDORA are the real self-report checks.
- LLM pilots are small (25 profiles, 100 essays); the full voter run is one sample per profile.
- Several effects (social 5, core 1, rare types) rest on groups of 20-70 profiles.
